My Hero - Makoto Shinkai

Interests Hero

Makoto Shinkai is a visionary Japanese animator and filmmaker. His present anime with his breathtaking artwork and emotionally resonant stories. He start his career with making short films independently soon he find out is impossible to make great anime by only himself. He keep produsing anime and learn how to work with others. The thing I like about his anime is the ending of his story mostly we won't say is a happy ending but you can feel the story is completed and end in the best way.

Your Name. (2016)
Written and directed by Shinkai Makoto; a blockbuster film about two high school students who body-swap across time and space.
Weathering with You (2019)
My Favorite. Written and directed by Shinkai Makoto; tells the story of a runaway boy who meets a girl with the power to control the weather in Tokyo.
Suzume (2022)
Written and directed by Shinkai Makoto; follows a young girl journeying across Japan to close mysterious doors preventing natural disasters.
